# Image assets

Three editorial images were created for this project using the **built-in image-generation tool**, then encoded as WebP for local use. They depict imagined subjects and do not document actual news events. No remote image downloads are required at runtime. Original UI-kit artwork is not included.

| Bundled path | Subject |
| --- | --- |
| `public/images/city.webp` and `dist/images/city.webp` | An imagined elevated urban park in warm evening light |
| `public/images/technology.webp` and `dist/images/technology.webp` | A fictional processor on a circuit board |
| `public/images/culture.webp` and `dist/images/culture.webp` | An imagined art-museum atrium with a curved staircase |

## Generation prompts

### City

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape editorial photograph for a news reading demo app. A beautiful elevated linear park winding through a dense modern city, lush trees and pedestrians, warm late afternoon natural light, brick facades mixed with glass towers, photojournalistic architectural composition, sophisticated green and warm cream color palette, wide 16:9 composition, sharp photographic detail. Imagined city, no identifiable landmark, no text, no logos, no collage, no watermarks.

### Technology

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide 16:9 editorial photo for a demo technology article. Close-up of a carefully designed futuristic processor on a dark circuit board with elegant turquoise and warm gold highlights, photoreal macro photography, shallow depth of field, believable engineered detail, luxurious editorial aesthetic, no letters no words no brand no logo, not a diagram.

### Culture

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape 16:9 editorial photograph for a demo culture article. Beautiful sunlit contemporary art museum atrium with warm terracotta walls, sculptural white curved staircase, a few people experiencing abstract art, composition emphasizing the architecture, calm editorial travel photography, natural realistic textures and light, no identifiable real museum, no text no logos no watermarks.
